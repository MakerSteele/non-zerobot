Flip these while a clip plays to find the tinny sound. Normal = 62 µs OFF, DITHER ON. Not saved - a restart puts them back to normal.
SMOOTH 0 = crisp and buzzy (the first version), 10 = round and a bit quieter. CLARITY cuts more bass and lifts the treble for crisper words (it does not save power - POWER LIMIT does that). 0 = the old sound.
Changes apply right away, even mid-sentence.
POWER LIMIT caps how hard the motors are pushed on average. Too high and the battery sags until the Pi reboots - raise it a step at a time while watching the battery.
LOUDNESS 6 = loud and clear. Above 7 the robot starts to twitch more than it gets louder.
